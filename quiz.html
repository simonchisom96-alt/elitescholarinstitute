FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

I paid but didn’t receive a passcode

Message us again on WhatsApp with proof of payment — a screenshot or transaction reference is all we need — and we'll send your passcode over immediately, no extra charge and no long wait. Delays are almost always just a missed message on our end, never a sign that something's wrong with your payment, so there's no need to panic or assume you've lost your money. Every payment is tracked against your account, so even if a first message gets buried, following up with proof gets it sorted fast. This is the only support step you'll ever need for a passcode issue — no forms, no tickets, no runaround, just a direct message and a direct fix.

What is Multiplayer mode and what features does it include?

Multiplayer unlocks the full competitive arena — play live against friends or scholars from around the globe, send 1v1 duel challenges, join full Team Battles, compete in hosted tournaments with live chat, and climb a global leaderboard that pays out prizes to the top three every month. Add friend requests, public profiles with avatar customization, a permanent Hall of Fame for the best of the best and tons of more features awaiting you — guaranteed to be the most complete competitive mode available, without compromise.

How does the ELO ranking system work?

Every ranked match moves your ELO using the same rating math used in competitive chess. Beat someone rated near or above you and you gain real points; beat someone far weaker and you barely gain anything, since you were expected to win anyway. There are 15 tiers in total, from Bronze all the way up to Elite Master, and each tier takes a bigger climb than the last — so the higher you go, the more it actually means. Casual matches and Team Battles never touch your ELO, so there's always a way to play risk-free.

What do I earn from playing?

Every match earns you coins and XP regardless of the outcome. Coins are spent in the Shop on exclusive cosmetic titles and effects, while XP permanently levels you up over time. On top of that, there's a full set of achievement badges to unlock — from your first win to hot streaks, tournament championships, and team MVP performances — all shown proudly on your public profile.

How long does Multiplayer stay unlocked?

Once unlocked on a device, it stays unlocked permanently on that device — there is no subscription, no monthly fee, and no expiry date attached to it. You pay once, and from that point forward every feature in Multiplayer — ranked matches, tournaments, team battles, the shop, everything — is yours for as long as you keep using that device. This is a deliberate one-time-purchase model, not a recurring one, because access to a mode you've already paid for shouldn't ever need to be paid for twice. The only time you'd need a new passcode is if you move to a different phone entirely, which is covered in the next question.

Can I use the same passcode on another phone?

No — each passcode is tied to a single device and can only be redeemed once, which is what keeps unlock codes fair and prevents them from being shared or resold. If you get a new phone or switch devices, your old passcode won't carry over automatically, but that's not something you need to worry about losing money over: just message us on WhatsApp, and a new unlock passcode will be issued to you for your new device. The one-device rule exists purely to protect the integrity of the unlock system, not to make life difficult for genuine players who've already paid — switching devices is a quick, straightforward fix, not a repurchase.
